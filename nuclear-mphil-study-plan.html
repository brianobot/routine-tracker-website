How this plan is built

The order isn't arbitrary — it's the same sequencing used to build the courses in the app: a precalculus refresher, then the calculus prerequisite, then four engineering-math foundations, then three physics/engineering bridge courses, then the seven-course Cambridge Nuclear Energy MPhil core (NE1–NE9), each capstone closing a question an earlier course opened.

Pace

~1 region / day

Rhythm

Weekdays: new material · Weekends: quests + review

Total scope

16 courses · 446 lesson nodes

Quest style

By-hand math, not code-heavy (except Numerical Methods & Computational Modelling)

Each course is organised into 7 regions (Calculus has 9, the new Precalculus refresher has 5) — a week at the standard 7-day pace is roughly one region a day. The four bigger/harder courses (Calculus, Differential Equations, Computational Reactor Modelling, and the final Advanced Fission & Fusion capstone) get 10 days instead of 7, so the pace there eases to under one region a day on purpose.

Honest tradeoff: the original plan fit exactly 15 courses into 4 months with a 2-day buffer at the end. Precalculus adds a genuine 7th day of content, and that buffer is what absorbed it — the plan below now runs to 5 Mar 2027, one week past "4 months from November," with no slack left at all. If you'd rather stay strictly inside 4 months, the cleanest cut is dropping one of the four 10-day courses back to 7 — Differential Equations and Computational Reactor Modelling are the two that tolerate that best. Otherwise, treat the extra week as the honest cost of the new prerequisite, not a rounding error.

Two course-weeks land on the winter holidays (flagged below, now on Nuclear & Particle Physics and Thermodynamics instead of their original slots). Shift freely; nothing in the app enforces a deadline, and progress per course is saved independently, so skipping ahead or circling back costs nothing.
